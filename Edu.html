This space is part of a project I have always wanted to get off the ground. I am not the best teacher in the world, but I believe that all knowledge is valid and should be shared. Here, I will organize the content by topics in a logical order. If you already master any of the listed technologies, you can simply skip that step, although they integrate with each other. At the end of each page, I have left a list of recommended readings.

Do not limit yourself to studying only through this website or platform: this is just the tip of the iceberg, and it is up to you to dive deeper and better understand each subject. If you want to contribute something or support this project, please contact me by email here. Some links will not work yet because the course hasn't started or because they are not ready yet. I started this project because I believe that education is a right for all and should not be restricted only to people with access to formal opportunities. Passing a college entrance exam or not shouldn't limit anyone from having access to a better education.
